Green = internals and a card agree, a trade may be on; Amber = something is set up but not confirmed — watch, don't act; Red = stand aside; Grey = market closed or no data. The checklist below shows exactly which conditions are met. Price breaking a level is always the trigger — never this light by itself.

    Internals

    The market's "vital signs" from the NYSE tape. TICK = stocks upticking minus downticking right now (±900 is extreme). TRIN = where the volume is going: above 1.5 the volume is in sellers, below 0.6 in buyers — but during broad rallies TRIN can sit above 1 because the big-cap volume dominates; that's "rotation," not weakness. ADD = advancing minus declining stocks. VOLD = up-volume minus down-volume. ADSPD = the same breadth count inside the S&P 500 only.

    Internals trend (gates) last 30 minutes

    Instead of reading one tick, we look at the direction of the last 30 minutes. If breadth (ADD) is building and either volume (VOLD) is building or TRIN is falling, the long gate is open — the tape agrees with buyers. The mirror opens the short gate. "FLAT" means the move is too small to matter. No open gate = no day trade, no matter how good the chart looks. Needs 15+ minutes of live data; otherwise it says UNRELIABLE.

    Trade cards state only — you pull the trigger

    The three plays that have actually worked (reaction trades, not predictions). Washout Long: TICK flushes to −900 or worse, then price reclaims the flush low → buy the reclaim. Cluster Fade: price reaches a liquidity pool while internals disagree with the move → fade it. Failed Retest: price breaks a level by a full 15-minute close, comes back to retest, and the retest fails → go with the break. A card that says ARMED still needs a gate open and your stop defined.

    Level ladder

    Every important price, from every method, on one ruler. pool = where stops are resting (liquidity); fvg = an unfilled price gap; conf = volume-profile / fibonacci confluence; swing = recent swing high/low; session = VWAP / opening range; TicToc = newsletter level (dated); meter = the engine's suggested entry/stop/targets. Nothing here is a trade until price actually breaks or holds the level.

    Meter

    An opinion poll of 11 signals (structure, breadth, confluence, sector, VIX…) for each timeframe: green share says LONG, red says SHORT, grey says HOLD. It is context, not a trigger. When it prints an A+ guidance block, those are suggested levels — still confirm against the ladder and the gate.
    Signals behind the meter

    MES / futures

    Hours-hold futures ideas. "Stand down" is a real answer — most days have no A+ level-break. When a setup shows, tap Copy to put entry / stop / target on the clipboard for ToS. Half size on event days (Fed, CPI, mega-cap earnings).

    Stocks — today's A+

    Only the setup types that have measured edge are shown as actionable: convergence-HIGH (65% win, +0.47R over 81 trades), PULLBACK_LONG and BOUNCE_SHORT. Ideas tagged "informational" (Minervini reset, ORB A+) are shadows still being measured — fine to study, size small if at all. Names that are extended (RSI pinned, far above the 50-day) are hidden so you don't chase.

    Gold / crude (trial)

    Gold EMA signals have real edge in testing (9/21 on 60-minute bars +11 pts per trade over 113 trades). Crude does not — it's ~0 after costs. Prices here are delayed 10–15 minutes; confirm on ToS before acting.

    How to use this page (60 seconds)

    1. Look at the light. Green = internals and a card agree, a trade may be on. Amber = set up but unconfirmed. Red = stand aside. Grey = closed / no data.

    2. Read the checklist. Every box must be ✓ before you take a day trade: live feed · gate open · a card armed · price at a ladder level · stop defined · not an event day.

    3. Use the two nearest levels. "Next above / next below" are the prices that matter in the next hour. The full ladder is one tap away.

    4. SETUPS lists only ideas with measured edge, with Copy buttons for ToS. "Stand down" is a normal answer.

    5. EDGE is the scoreboard — what works, what doesn't, what's too early to say. If a strategy isn't under "What's working," don't trade it.

    6. Tap "? Explain" any time to turn on plain-English notes under every section.

    Internals confirm. Price triggers. Cycle dates are windows, not switches.
